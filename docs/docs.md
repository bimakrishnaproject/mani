1. Purpose of This Document
The current creative direction must be abandoned and rebuilt from the ground up.
This document establishes a complete visual, strategic, editorial, UX, motion and page-structure
framework for the new MANI website. It should be treated as the primary design reference
before any new interface work begins.
The objective is not to reinterpret the previous layouts or make minor visual adjustments. The
objective is to create a completely new digital direction that accurately reflects the brand
materials, content, products, app and expectations supplied by the MANI team.
The final website must feel:
Premium
Editorial
Modern
Intelligent
Emotionally aware
Calm but visually powerful
Human without becoming sentimental
Minimal without feeling empty
Sophisticated without feeling inaccessible
Innovative without relying on visual gimmicks
The experience should feel like the intersection of:
Apple’s product clarity, a premium contemporary magazine and an advanced digital
editorial platform.
It must not look like:
A therapy practice
A healthcare platform
A wellness retreat
A meditation application
A coaching website
A traditional self-help brand
A generic Shopify storefront
A corporate SaaS platform
The source content defines MANI as a platform bringing together collections, daily educational
videos, expert guidance, community and an app currently in beta. The website structure must
make those areas feel like one connected ecosystem rather than unrelated products and pages.
2. Brand Foundation
2.1 Core Positioning
MANI helps people better understand themselves and navigate life’s challenges through:
Expert-led content
Structured physical and digital collections
Short educational videos
Community
Guided technology
A future application
The core promise is:
Emotional Support Made Simple™
This statement should not be interpreted as “soft,” “basic” or “simplistic.”
The brand simplifies access and understanding while preserving the intelligence and complexity
of the subject matter.
MANI does not diagnose, treat or position itself as a substitute for professional mental-health
care. Its role is educational, informational and supportive. That distinction must be visible in the
brand tone and appropriately communicated in the legal and footer areas.
2.2 Central Brand Idea
The entire experience should be organised around one conceptual progression:
Understand → Reflect → Apply → Move Forward
This progression connects every MANI oﬀering:
Watch & Learn creates understanding.
Collections create deeper reflection and practical application.
The App creates accessible guided support.
Community creates continuity and connection.
The website should never feel like a catalogue of isolated services.
It should feel like one intelligent support system.
2.3 Emotional Experience
A visitor should feel:
1. Recognised
“This platform understands what people actually experience.”
2. Reassured
“I am not being judged, diagnosed or sold a false promise.”
3. Interested
“This is a fresh, intelligent way to explore emotional well-being.”
4. Guided
“I understand where to go next.”
5. Empowered
“I can take a practical next step.”
The design should acknowledge diﬃcult emotional experiences without visually dwelling on
suﬀering.
Avoid imagery, language or motion that makes visitors feel fragile, ill, broken or clinically
assessed.
3. Creative Concept
3.1 Recommended Concept Name
The Living Editorial System
MANI should behave like a digital publication that is alive, responsive and continuously evolving.
The visual language should combine:
Editorial grids
Strong typography
Carefully framed product compositions
Selective app interface moments
Video-led content
Human expertise
Smooth spatial transitions
Modular content architecture
The experience should feel highly designed, but never overdesigned.
3.2 The Creative Contrast
The visual identity should be built around a deliberate contrast:
Grounded Progressive
Deep green Bright white
Trust Innovation
Editorial clarity Advanced motion
Human language Digital precision
Emotional warmth Structural restraint
Timeless typography Contemporary interaction
This contrast is essential.
Using only green, cream and soft visuals would push MANI toward a predictable wellness
identity.
Using only stark white, black and sharp technology visuals would make it feel clinical or
corporate.
The new direction must exist between these two extremes.

Do Not
Build the website on beige or yellow-toned backgrounds.

6.3 Typography Rules
Headlines should be concise and large.
Body copy must remain readable and never become excessively narrow.
Use typography as an architectural element.
Avoid centring every section.
Alternate between centred emotional statements and left-aligned editorial content.
Keep paragraphs relatively short.
Use generous space before and after key messages.
Avoid decorative italic text except for carefully selected quotations.
Do not use oversized type purely for visual impact if it harms comprehension.

9. Motion and Interaction System
Motion should be one of the defining characteristics of the website, but every movement must
support orientation, storytelling or comprehension.
9.1 Global Motion Principles
Smooth
Controlled
Quietly impressive
Purposeful
Lightweight
Responsive
Premium
9.2 Recommended Motion Types
Page Entry
A minimal mask or fade transition using the circular MANI identity.
Duration: approximately 500–800 ms.
Typography Reveals
Lines or words reveal vertically with slight stagger.
No excessive letter-by-letter eﬀects on long paragraphs.
Image Reveals
Images appear through clipping masks, soft scale or controlled parallax.
Product Motion
Products may separate gently from a grouped composition as the visitor scrolls.
Video Hover
Muted preview or a subtle thumbnail movement.
App Conversation
Messages may reveal progressively as the user reaches the section.
Section Transitions
Background changes should feel spatial and intentional.
Navigation
The header should transition from transparent to solid or blurred only when needed for
readability.
9.3 Motion to Avoid
Constant floating objects
Excessive 3D rotation
Long loading sequences
Aggressive horizontal movement
Multiple competing parallax eﬀects
Gimmicky cursor eﬀects
Decorative particles
Liquid wellness animations
Random gradient movement
Shaky text
Scroll-jacking that removes user control
Slow transitions that obstruct access to information
All animation must have a reduced-motion alternative.

The redesigned MANI website should feel like a sophisticated, future-facing publication and

Editorial layouts
Premium product composition
Meaningful motion
Intelligent content organisation
Human expertise
Clear UX
Structural simplicity
The final experience should feel emotionally warm without becoming sentimental,
