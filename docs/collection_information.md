Manasa founded Mani after experiencing firsthand what it feels like to need support and struggle to find it. That experience stayed with her. As she learned, listened, and spoke with others, she realized that countless people were facing life's challenges without the support they needed. When she finally found the guidance, expertise, and support that helped her, she became committed to making that same quality of support more accessible to others.

Mani was created from a simple belief: emotional support should be easier to access, easier to understand, and available when people need it most. What began as a personal experience evolved into a mission to help more people access trusted guidance, practical tools, and meaningful support during life's most challenging moments. Today, that belief continues to guide everything Mani creates**.**
