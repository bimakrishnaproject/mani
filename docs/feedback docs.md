Realigning the Homepage With Provided User Journey
Before reviewing the individual sections below, there is one
overarching issue to address.
We previously provided clear direction for the homepage
structure, user journey, and narrative flow, and these were
intended to form the foundation of the website design.
Instead, many sections were reorganized, merged, duplicated, or
replaced, resulting in unnecessary repetition, competing navigation
patterns, and a user journey that no longer follows the intended
progression.
The feedback below should not be viewed as isolated design
comments. The primary objective is to realign the homepage with the
structure and flow we provided. Once that foundation has been
restored, the individual design refinements will have much greater
impact.

Creative Direction Summary
The redesign should focus on three principles:
● Preserve the intended user journey rather than creating a new narrative.
● Reduce cognitive load by simplifying navigation, removing repetition, and
eliminating competing visual elements.
● Ensure every section has a distinct purpose that moves the story forward.
The detailed feedback below explains how these principles apply throughout the
homepage.

The Experience We Are Trying to Create

MANI is an emotional support platform. Many people visiting the website may already
feel overwhelmed, emotionally exhausted, anxious, or stuck in overthinking. The
website itself should begin reducing that cognitive and emotional load from the very first
screen.
Every design decision should support that objective. The experience should feel calm
rather than stimulating, simple rather than busy, soft rather than sharp, spacious rather
than crowded, and guiding rather than demanding.
Users should never feel they are processing too much information at once. Navigation
should feel effortless, typography should be warm and highly legible, and motion should
be subtle and purposeful. Each section should introduce one clear idea before naturally
leading into the next, creating a calm, uninterrupted experience.
The goal is simple: visitors should feel calmer after thirty seconds on the homepage
than they did when they arrived. Every design decision should support that outcome.

Overall Homepage Structure
The homepage currently repeats the same concepts through different headlines and
layouts. Rather than building the story, several sections reintroduce the MANI
ecosystem from different angles, making the experience feel longer and more repetitive
than necessary.
The homepage should first establish what MANI is before introducing individual
collections or products. Users should understand the platform and how the ecosystem
works before being asked to explore its components.
Once that foundation has been established, each major idea should be introduced once
and then expanded upon as users move through the homepage. Every section should
have a distinct purpose that moves the story forward rather than restating information
already introduced.
Please review the homepage as one complete experience rather than as individual
sections. Improving isolated components will not solve the overall issue if the overall
flow remains fragmented or repetitive.

1. Global Typography

Typography should reinforce the MANI experience by creating clarity, warmth, and
consistency throughout the homepage. It should guide the eye naturally from one
section to the next without competing for attention.
The current typography creates a strong visual identity, but it is used too consistently in
scale and emphasis across the homepage. Large editorial headlines appear repeatedly,
giving many sections equal visual weight. Rather than creating a clear rhythm, the
typography competes with itself. Greater variation in scale, hierarchy, and pacing would
allow it to support the experience instead of dominating it.

2. Logo (see public/assets/Mani Logo folder)

The approved MANI logo was provided and should be used exactly as supplied.
The current header uses the wrong logo. Please replace it with the approved logo and
use it consistently throughout the website.

3. Navigation (see public/assets/feedback/feedback1.png)

Navigation should feel effortless, intuitive, and almost invisible. Users should move
naturally through the homepage without constantly being asked to click, switch views, or
reorient themselves. The experience should feel calm, guided, and easy to follow.
The current navigation feels jumpy, fragmented, and overly interactive. Throughout the
homepage, users are repeatedly asked to scroll, click, switch between views, and
process multiple navigation patterns before they have fully understood the content.
Rather than creating a smooth, continuous journey, the experience feels busy and
requires too much effort. Every interaction should have a clear purpose, guiding users
naturally from one section to the next instead of repeatedly asking them to choose
where to go.

4. Hero Section (see public/assets/feedback/feedback2.png and see public/assets/feedback/feedback3.png)

The hero should communicate one clear idea and immediately establish what MANI is.
It should introduce the homepage in the way the original user journey intended, then
naturally lead users into the next section.
The current hero does not follow the intended flow. It introduces additional concepts,
including the Core Beliefs, before the primary message has been established. The
oversized headline, rotating Core Beliefs, floating labels, supporting copy, multiple calls
to action, and promotional badges compete for attention, making it difficult to know
where to look first.
The hero should focus on one clear message and allow the remaining content to unfold
in the sequence of the original homepage structure.

5. Your MANI Ecosystem AND One System, Multiple Ways of
Support (see public/assets/feedback/feedback4.png)

This section does not follow the homepage structure we originally provided. The current
homepage introduces the MANI ecosystem twice through Bye Bye Narcissist Collection
and One System, Multiple Ways of Support, creating repetition instead of progression.

The homepage should introduce the ecosystem once before naturally moving users
forward.
The presentation also feels flat and lacks engagement. The product photography
appears static rather than interactive, and the books, videos, and app feel like separate
pieces instead of one connected experience. The layouts do not create curiosity or
invite exploration, and the interaction lacks the movement and craftsmanship expected
from a modern digital experience.
This section should feel dynamic, intuitive, and immersive, helping users immediately
understand how every part of the MANI ecosystem works together while encouraging
them to continue exploring.

6. Brand Philosophy
his section does not follow the homepage structure we originally provided. The
Brand Philosophy should create a meaningful pause within the homepage, giving
visitors an opportunity to connect with the thinking behind MANI.
The philosophy itself is strong, but its impact is reduced because similar
messages appear throughout the homepage. Rather than reinforcing the
message, the repetition weakens it. This should remain the primary place where
the philosophy is introduced, allowing it to stand on its own instead of competing
with similar messaging elsewhere.

7. Watch & Learn (see public/assets/feedback/feedback5.png)

Watch & Learn is a core part of the MANI ecosystem and should be promoted on the
homepage as such. It should clearly communicate that users have access to expert
guidance, education, and practical tools that extend beyond the physical collections.
Watch & Learn is largely missing from the homepage experience. It should have its own
dedicated section that clearly communicates its purpose, value, and relationship to the
collections and app, reinforcing that it is one of the primary ways users receive support
through MANI.

8. Featured Collection (see public/assets/feedback/feedback6.png)

This section does not follow the homepage structure we originally provided. The
overall concept is strong, but the execution introduces too much visual and
cognitive complexity. Multiple navigation options, calls to action, product
information, pricing, badges, tabs, and large product imagery compete for
attention, making it difficult to know where to look or what action to take.
The interaction has potential, but the experience feels busy rather than intuitive.
The product photography feels static, the interface feels crowded, and every
element competes equally for attention. Instead of creating curiosity and
encouraging exploration, the section overwhelms users rather than guiding them.

8. App (see public/assets/feedback/feedback7.png)

This section does not follow the homepage structure we originally provided. The app is
a core part of the MANI ecosystem and should be presented within that ecosystem
rather than as a separate, repeated section. Each major part of the ecosystem should
be introduced once, then the homepage should continue telling the story.
Beyond the repetition, the presentation feels visually busy without creating meaningful
engagement. Multiple floating labels, navigation elements, chat examples, badges, and
interface components compete for attention, making it difficult to know where to look.
Rather than demonstrating the app experience, the layout feels like a collection of
disconnected interface elements.
The app is one of MANI's most important products and should feel alive. Instead of
static graphics, it should communicate real-time interaction and emotional support
through thoughtful motion and purposeful animation. Every visual element should help
users immediately understand what the app does rather than requiring them to interpret
the interface.

9. Newsletter

The newsletter and footer currently merge into one continuous section, despite serving
different purposes. The newsletter invites users to continue their journey, while the
footer provides navigation, legal information, and supporting resources. Because both
share the same visual treatment, the newsletter loses its impact and the end of the
homepage feels heavier than it should.
These sections should be visually distinct, allowing the newsletter to stand on its own
before transitioning naturally into the footer.

10. Footer

The footer contains the right information, but because it visually blends into the
newsletter above, it creates one long section that feels heavy and overwhelming.
Separating the footer from the newsletter will improve the pacing of the homepage and
allow each section to serve its own purpose.
The footer should feel like a quiet conclusion to the experience, not a continuation of the
primary content.
Overall, the website contains many strong ideas. The opportunity is to simplify the
experience, remove repetition, strengthen hierarchy, and create a homepage that feels
calm, intuitive, and emotionally reassuring. Every section should have a clear purpose,
every interaction should feel intentional, and the experience should unfold naturally from
beginning to end.
