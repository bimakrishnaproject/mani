**BRAND OVERVIEW**

MANI helps people better understand themselves and navigate life's challenges through expert-led content, videos, community, and technology.

The MANI brand should feel intelligent, modern, trustworthy, and deeply human.

* We are not building a therapy website.  
* We are not building a healthcare platform.  
* We are not building a meditation app.  
* We are not building a coaching website.  
* We are not building a traditional self-help brand.

We are building a modern support platform designed for the future. Every design decision should support one simple idea: **Emotional Support Made Simple.**

---

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

**DESIGN VISION**

**MANI should feel like the intersection of Apple and a premium magazine publication.**

The experience should feel:

• Modern  
• Sophisticated  
• Intelligent  
• Calm  
• Human  
• Credible  
• Timeless

The website should feel intentionally designed, with a strong focus on simplicity, clarity, and quality.

The experience should communicate confidence through restraint rather than complexity and should feel as relevant five years from now as it does today.

---

**VISUAL DIRECTION**

The website should feel closer to a modern digital publication than a traditional marketing website.

**We value:**

* Strong visual hierarchy  
* Editorial layouts  
* Sophisticated typography  
* Generous whitespace  
* Bold visual storytelling  
* Elegant motion design  
* High-end user experience  
* Seamless transitions  
* Thoughtful interactions  
* Precision and restraint

Avoid:

* Generic wellness aesthetics  
* Therapy website design  
* Healthcare website design  
* Self-help clichés  
* Corporate SaaS design patterns  
* Trendy design fads  
* Visual clutter  
* Decorative design elements  
* Excessive gradients  
* Excessive shadows  
* Overly rounded interfaces  
* Stock wellness imagery

**If someone removed the logo, the website should still feel unmistakably like MANI.**

**COLOR SYSTEM**

**Primary Brand Color**

Deep Green  
\#0E2E1E

**This color should remain the visual anchor of the brand.** It represents trust, grounding, confidence, intelligence, and stability.

**Background Direction**

The website should primarily use:

* White  
* Soft white  
* Very light neutral tones

The experience should feel bright, spacious, and modern.

**Avoid:**

* Cream-heavy layouts  
* Beige backgrounds  
* Yellow-toned neutrals  
* Vintage color palettes  
* Earthy wellness aesthetics

**Supporting Colors**

Supporting colors should feel:

* Modern  
* Sophisticated  
* Minimal  
* Timeless

Supporting colors should enhance the green rather than compete with it. Example white and black&nbsp;

---

**TYPOGRAPHY**

Typography is one of the most important elements of the MANI brand.

The typography system should feel:

* Editorial  
* Sophisticated  
* Modern  
* Intelligent  
* Highly readable

The website should feel closer to a premium publication than a wellness brand.

**Roca Usage:** Roca should be used exclusively for the MANI logo.

Roca should never appear in:

* Headlines  
* Subheadings  
* Body copy  
* Navigation  
* Buttons  
* Interface elements  
* Website content

The website should not be designed around the existing logo typography.

**Font Selection**

Typography should prioritize:

* Clarity  
* Sophistication  
* Readability  
* Modernity

The final typography system should support the overall design vision of the brand.

---

**MOTION & INTERACTION**

Motion should be a defining part of the MANI experience.

Motion should never feel decorative.&nbsp;

Every animation should have a purpose and help guide the user experience.

The experience should feel closer to moving through a thoughtfully designed digital experience than navigating a traditional website.

**We want:**

• Smooth transitions  
• Elegant reveals  
• Thoughtful use of parallax  
• Seamless page movement  
• Layered storytelling  
• Refined micro-interactions  
• Dynamic visual moments  
• Sophisticated scroll experiences

Visitors should feel that MANI belongs to the next generation of digital experiences.

**Avoid:**

* Gimmicky animations  
* Slow-loading effects  
* Distracting movement  
* Motion for the sake of motion

Every animation should have a purpose.

---

**PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY**

We want to avoid stock photography and traditional lifestyle imagery.

Please use only assets provided by the MANI team, including:

• Product images  
• Collection mockups  
• App screenshots  
• Team photos  
• Brand assets

If additional imagery is needed, please discuss it with us before sourcing or creating new visuals.

---

**USER EXPERIENCE**

The experience should feel effortless, intuitive, and easy to understand.

The design should create clarity rather than overwhelm.

The website should feel premium because it is simple, not because it is complicated.

Every page and section should serve a clear purpose.

---

**BRAND VOICE**

The voice of MANI should be:

• Clear  
• Human  
• Thoughtful  
• Intelligent  
• Supportive  
• Honest  
• Grounded

**The voice should never feel:**

• Clinical  
• Corporate  
• Fear-based  
• Sales-driven  
• Transactional

We acknowledge that life can be difficult without dwelling on pain.

We meet people where they are while helping them move forward.
