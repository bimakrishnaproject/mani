Watch & Learn
On the homepage Watch & Learn card, the featured video should be the newest video from
the Watch & Learn content tab. The videos are currently being pulled in the wrong order, which
is causing older content to appear first. Please reorder the Watch & Learn content from newest
to oldest so the homepage card always displays the most recent video, and the full Watch &
Learn page follows the same order.
Each video card should clearly display the approved title, thumbnail, and caption or summary so
users can understand the topic before opening the video. The video card on the homepage
does not do this.
Additionally, The Watch & Learn section of the website should be organized using the structure
provided in the content document. The first hashtag for each video identifies its primary topic
and should be used as the category. Users should be able to browse videos by topic, rather
than viewing a list of videos. The approved titles, captions, thumbnails, and categories should
be used throughout.
